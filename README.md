# AI Assurance Signals

A weekly, source-grounded digest of AI assurance and governance developments — Australia first — for risk & compliance professionals, AI practitioners, and executives/boards.

- Site: https://aisignals.willbright.link (coming soon)
- Published every Friday afternoon (AEST/AEDT), fully automated from an allow-list of government, regulator, standards, frontier-lab and news sources.
- Summaries are AI-generated (Gemini) and verified against verbatim quotes from the source; always rely on the original.
